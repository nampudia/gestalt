How it works

From upload to notes in three steps.

  1. 01

    Upload your cut, pick your audience

    Choose who should see it by age, genre taste and whether they've seen the trailer. Every viewer gets a personal, watermarked invite that expires.

  2. 02

    They watch on their own screens

    The camera notices laughs, gasps, tension, confusion and drifting. Analysis runs on the viewer's device and no video is saved. Viewers are paid only for complete, verified watches.

  3. 03

    Get the report and audience notes

    A scene-by-scene timeline of your film, a short survey broken down by audience group, and AI-written notes ranked by impact, each linked to its evidence.

Scene by scene

Markers for the edit

Audience notes, written for the edit room.

After every screening, AI reads the whole timeline, every scene and every survey answer, and writes the notes a seasoned research analyst would: ranked by impact, backed by the numbers, and upfront about small samples.

They're notes from your audience, not orders. Every claim links to the frame it came from, so you can check it in seconds.

For your teams

One screening, three decisions made better.

Directors and editors

Make the cut

  • Which scenes to trim, and how much time it buys back.
  • Which jokes landed, which missed, and which laughs bury the next line.
  • Where people got lost, and what they didn't follow.
  • Cut 3 against cut 4, side by side.

Marketing

Sell the film

  • The audience that loves it, so ad spend goes where it lands.
  • The strongest moments for trailers and spots, ranked by group.
  • Peak moments ready to cut as vertical social clips.
  • Taglines grounded in how viewers describe it.

Distribution

Release it right

  • Theatrical, streaming or festival, read from real pay intent.
  • Word-of-mouth signals: recommend rate and what stuck.
  • Benchmarks against comparable films as the library grows.
  • A clear read on who to screen next, and how many.

Security and privacy

Your unreleased cut stays yours.

Built for films nobody is supposed to see yet, and for viewers who deserve to know exactly what's measured.

Every viewer watermarked

Their name and email move across the frame, so any leak traces to one person.

Locked playback

Personal links that expire. No downloads, no skipping, and the film pauses when a viewer leaves the tab.

DRM playbackRoadmap

Studio-grade DRM so screen recordings come out black.

No video of viewers, ever

Face analysis runs on the viewer's own device. Only per-second numbers leave it: attention, blinks and expression strength.

Consent first, verified watches

Adult viewers agree to confidentiality and to being measured before they press play, and are paid only when they finish with their eyes on screen.
